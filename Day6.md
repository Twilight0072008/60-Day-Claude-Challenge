# Resume Optimization & ATS Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this exercise was to analyze an existing resume, optimize it for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), compare the results, and identify the improvements that increased the ATS score.

---

# Original Resume Overview

## Initial ATS Score

**38/100**

### Characteristics of the Original Resume

* No Professional Summary section
* Non-standard section headings
* Skills were not categorized
* Misspelled keyword ("Phyton" instead of "Python")
* Canva incorrectly listed as a framework
* Project descriptions lacked action verbs
* Contact information was not ATS-friendly
* Included vague and redundant content

### Limitations

* Lower keyword relevance
* Poor ATS readability
* Weak project descriptions
* Reduced recruiter impact
* Lower chances of passing automated screening systems

---

# Optimized Resume Overview

## Optimized ATS Score

**78/100**

### Improvements Made

* Added a Professional Summary
* Used ATS-friendly section headings
* Organized skills into categories
* Corrected technical keyword spelling
* Removed incorrectly categorized skills
* Enhanced project descriptions
* Added ATS-friendly contact details
* Reduced unnecessary content

### Benefits

* Improved keyword matching
* Better ATS parsing
* Increased recruiter readability
* Stronger project presentation
* More professional structure

---

# ATS Improvement Analysis

## 1. Added a Professional Summary

### What Changed

Added a concise summary highlighting:

* Web Development
* HTML
* CSS
* CSE Background

### Why It Improved the Score

ATS systems scan the top section of a resume first. Including role-specific keywords immediately improves keyword density and relevance.

---

## 2. Fixed Section Heading Labels

### What Changed

Replaced non-standard headings with:

* EDUCATION
* PROJECTS
* SKILLS
* CERTIFICATIONS

### Why It Improved the Score

ATS parsers recognize standard section names and can correctly categorize information.

---

## 3. Structured Skills into Categories

### What Changed

Skills were grouped under:

* Programming Languages
* Frontend Technologies
* Tools & Software

### Why It Improved the Score

Categorized skills improve ATS extraction and help recruiters quickly identify technical competencies.

---

## 4. Corrected "Phyton" to "Python"

### What Changed

Corrected the spelling error.

### Why It Improved the Score

ATS systems rely heavily on exact keyword matching. Misspelled skills often go undetected.

---

## 5. Removed Canva from Frameworks

### What Changed

Removed Canva from the Frameworks section.

### Why It Improved the Score

Canva is a design tool, not a framework. Correct categorization improves accuracy and credibility.

---

## 6. Rewrote Project Descriptions Using Action Verbs

### What Changed

Changed descriptions from passive statements to:

* Designed and developed...
* Implemented...
* Applied...

### Why It Improved the Score

Action-oriented language demonstrates ownership, initiative, and practical experience.

---

## 7. Added Plain-Text Contact Information

### What Changed

Included:

* Email
* Phone Number
* Location

At the top of the resume.

### Why It Improved the Score

ATS systems can reliably extract contact information when presented in plain text.

---

## 8. Removed Redundant Content

### What Changed

Removed vague extracurricular and repetitive information.

### Why It Improved the Score

Reducing unnecessary content increases keyword density for technical skills and improves readability.

---

# Key Learnings

### 1. ATS Optimization Is Critical

A technically strong resume may still perform poorly if it is not ATS-friendly.

### 2. Keywords Matter

Relevant technical keywords significantly impact ATS rankings.

### 3. Standard Formatting Improves Parsing

Using recognized section headings helps ATS systems accurately classify information.

### 4. Action Verbs Strengthen Projects

Strong verbs make projects appear more impactful and professional.

### 5. Categorized Skills Improve Visibility

Organized skill sections improve both ATS extraction and recruiter readability.

### 6. Small Errors Have Big Consequences

Even a minor spelling mistake such as "Phyton" can prevent important skills from being detected.

### 7. Quality Over Quantity

Removing irrelevant information can improve overall resume effectiveness.

---

# Conclusion

This exercise demonstrated how targeted resume optimization can significantly improve ATS performance. By focusing on keyword relevance, standard formatting, skill organization, and stronger project descriptions, the resume score increased from **38/100 to 78/100**, making it more competitive for internships and entry-level software development opportunities.
