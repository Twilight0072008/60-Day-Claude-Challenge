# 🚀 Day 27 – Prior Authorization Story Simulator

## 📌 Overview

As part of **Day 27 of the 60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I developed an interactive **Prior Authorization Story Simulator** using Claude AI. Unlike a traditional workflow simulator, this project presents the healthcare prior authorization process through a story-driven experience, allowing users to follow a patient's journey while learning each step of the insurance approval process.

The application combines storytelling, interactive decision-making, and educational content to simplify a complex healthcare workflow.

# 📖 Story Flow

The simulator follows **Rahul**, a patient diagnosed with Rheumatoid Arthritis, while **Priya**, a healthcare operations specialist, explains each step of the Prior Authorization process.

The story progresses through eight chapters:

1. Doctor Visit
2. Insurance Roadblock
3. Understanding Prior Authorization
4. Insurance Review Process
5. Denial Scenario
6. Appeal Process
7. Approval Outcome
8. Final Takeaways

Throughout the journey, users make decisions that influence dialogue while gaining a practical understanding of the authorization workflow.
