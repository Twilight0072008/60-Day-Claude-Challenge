# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint with Claude AI

## 📌 Objective

The objective of this challenge was to transform a validated startup idea into a structured **Customer & MVP Blueprint** using Claude AI. The focus was on understanding the target customer, prioritizing MVP features, identifying market risks, and creating a practical roadmap before product development.

---

# 🚀 Startup Overview

### Startup Name

**ClearLeaf**

### Domain

HealthTech • Wellness • Smoking Cessation

### Startup Idea

ClearLeaf is a science-backed smoking cessation solution designed to help smokers quit by addressing both nicotine dependence and the behavioral habit of smoking. The solution combines a nicotine-free herbal product with a companion application for craving tracking and progress monitoring.

---

# 🛠 Workflow

* Reviewed the Startup Validation Report.
* Extracted startup details and customer insights.
* Generated a Customer & MVP Blueprint using Claude AI.
* Analyzed the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
* Created Buyer Persona and Customer Journey.
* Reviewed customer pain points and buying triggers.
* Generated MVP recommendations and feature prioritization.
* Evaluated pricing strategy and business risks.
* Built a 30-Day MVP execution roadmap.
* Exported the final report and documented key learnings.

# 📚 Key Learnings

### ✅ Build for a Specific Customer

A clearly defined Ideal Customer Profile helps prioritize product features and marketing efforts.

### ✅ Solve One Core Problem First

An MVP should focus on validating the biggest customer pain point before expanding functionality.

### ✅ Prioritize Features Wisely

Using the MoSCoW framework prevents unnecessary development and keeps the product focused.

### ✅ Customer Feedback is Essential

Interviews, waitlists, and early adopters provide stronger validation than assumptions.

### ✅ AI Speeds Up Product Planning

Claude AI helped convert a startup idea into a structured product strategy, customer blueprint, and execution roadmap within minutes.

# 💡 Final Takeaway

A successful MVP is not about building every possible feature—it is about delivering the smallest solution that solves the customer's biggest problem. AI can accelerate customer research, product planning, and prioritization, but continuous user feedback is essential for achieving true Product–Market Fit.
