# Day 14 – AI Job Description Red Flag Detector

## Objective

The objective of this exercise was to use AI to critically analyze a Job Description (JD) and Company Information to identify potential risks before applying. Instead of evaluating a role based only on the job title or salary, the analysis focused on hiring practices, company culture, role expectations, and overall job quality.

---

# Task Overview

A Digital Marketing Intern job description and company profile were provided to AI for evaluation.

The AI analyzed:

* Job Requirements
* Company Information
* Work Culture
* Hiring Process
* Remote Work Claims
* Overall Hiring Risks

The output included a comprehensive risk assessment and practical recommendations for job seekers.

# Smart Interview Questions

To validate the role, AI generated the following questions:

1. How is success measured during the internship?
2. What does a typical workday look like for interns?
3. Will interns receive structured mentorship and feedback?
4. What percentage of interns receive full-time offers?
5. How are responsibilities distributed across the marketing team?

---

# Key Learnings

### 1. Every Job Description Should Be Evaluated Critically

A well-written job description should be transparent about expectations, compensation, and growth opportunities.

---

### 2. Company Culture Matters

Learning opportunities, mentorship, and work-life balance can be just as important as salary.

---

### 3. Not Every "Remote" Job Is Fully Remote

Checking for hidden relocation or office requirements is essential before applying.

---

### 4. AI Can Identify Hiring Risks Quickly

AI can highlight unrealistic expectations, vague requirements, and missing information that applicants may overlook.

---

### 5. Asking Smart Interview Questions Builds Confidence

Preparing targeted questions helps candidates better understand the role and make informed career decisions.
