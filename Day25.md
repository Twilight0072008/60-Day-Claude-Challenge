## 📌 Challenge Overview

Today's challenge was to build a complete **AI Shark Tank Simulator** using a single self-contained HTML application.

The objective was to simulate a realistic startup pitching experience where entrepreneurs can present their business ideas, answer investor questions, receive AI-generated feedback, and obtain an investment decision based on multiple evaluation criteria.

The application was designed with a modern dark-themed interface and runs entirely in the browser without requiring a backend.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

* Build a production-quality startup pitch simulator.
* Design multiple AI investor personas.
* Create an interactive Q&A experience.
* Implement a startup scoring system.
* Generate investment decisions dynamically.
* Build an attractive, responsive UI.
* Keep everything inside one standalone HTML file.

### Interactive Pitch Round

The simulator allows:

* Startup presentation
* AI-generated investor questions
* Founder responses
* Dynamic investor reactions
* Progressive evaluation

---

### Startup Scorecard

Each startup receives scores across:

* Market Potential
* Innovation
* Business Model
* Execution
* Investment Worthiness

Final score is calculated out of **100**.

---

### Investment Decision Engine

Based on the evaluation, the simulator generates one of the following outcomes:

* ✅ Invest
* ❌ Reject
* 🤝 Acquire
* ⏳ Come Back Later
