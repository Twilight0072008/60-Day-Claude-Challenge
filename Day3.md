# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## Objective

To understand how Role-Based Prompting changes the quality, perspective, and focus of AI-generated responses.

---

## Question Used

How can self-healing electronics be developed and commercialized in the future?

---

# Prompt 1: Without Role

## Prompt

```text
How can self-healing electronics be developed and commercialized in the future?
```

## Observation

The response provided a general overview of self-healing electronics, including applications, benefits, and future opportunities. The explanation was broad and lacked deep technical or business-specific insights.

---

# Prompt 2: Founder Persona

## Prompt

```text
Act as a deep-tech startup founder with experience in advanced materials and electronics.

How can self-healing electronics be developed and commercialized in the future?

Focus on:
- Market opportunities
- Business model
- Potential customers
- Funding opportunities
- Competitive advantages
- Go-to-market strategy
```

## Output Screenshot

## Observation

The response focused on commercialization, startup creation, funding opportunities, intellectual property, industry partnerships, and market strategy. The answer was business-oriented and focused on turning the technology into a viable company.

# Prompt 3: Electronics Engineer & Materials Scientist Persona

## Prompt

```text
Act as a senior electronics engineer and materials scientist specializing in self-healing electronics.

How can self-healing electronics be developed and commercialized in the future?

Focus on:
- Self-healing materials
- Circuit design
- Sensors
- Manufacturing challenges
- AI integration
- Technical roadmap
```

# Comparison of Responses

| Aspect         | No Role       | Founder Persona   | Engineer Persona        |
| -------------- | ------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| Perspective    | General       | Business          | Technical               |
| Focus          | Overview      | Commercialization | R&D and Engineering     |
| Audience       | General Users | Entrepreneurs     | Researchers & Engineers |
| Output Quality | Broad         | Strategic         | Technical & Detailed    |

---

# Key Learnings

1. Role-Based Prompting significantly changes AI responses.
2. Different personas provide different perspectives on the same problem.
3. Founder personas focus on business, funding, and commercialization.
4. Engineer personas focus on technology, materials, circuits, and implementation.
5. Role-Based Prompting helps generate more targeted and useful outputs.
6. Choosing the correct role improves the relevance and quality of AI-generated content.

---

# Conclusion

This activity demonstrated that Role-Based Prompting can transform AI from a general assistant into a domain-specific expert. By assigning different roles, the same question about self-healing electronics produced completely different insights, helping explore both commercialization and technical development perspectives.
