🚀 **Day 16/60 of My Claude AI Challenge – Building a Reusable AI Skill for Stock Fundamental Research**

Today, I explored how **Claude Custom Skills** can transform repetitive prompts into reusable AI workflows.

Instead of writing a detailed prompt every time I wanted to research a company, I created a custom skill called **`stock-fundamental-research`**.

To test it, I analyzed **ITC Ltd.** and generated a structured report that included:

📊 Company Snapshot
📈 Financial Performance
💰 Valuation Analysis
📉 Growth & Profitability Metrics
👥 Shareholding Trends
⚠️ Risk Factors
🏢 Business Quality & Competitive Advantage (MOAT)
📋 Overall Investment Summary *(for educational purposes only)*

### 💡 Biggest Learning

The value of AI isn't just in answering questions—it's in creating **reusable systems**.

With a Custom Skill, I can now analyze different companies by simply changing the company name. The workflow, structure, and report quality remain consistent without rewriting lengthy prompts.

### 📚 Key Takeaways

✅ Custom Skills save time by eliminating repetitive prompting.

✅ Structured reports make complex financial information easier to understand.

✅ A consistent framework helps compare multiple companies objectively.

✅ AI can accelerate research, but investment decisions should always be backed by official financial statements and independent analysis.
